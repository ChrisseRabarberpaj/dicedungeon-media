# Google Search test (from the 28 Oct marketing review): 100–120 € over 6 weeks, Helsinki metro, bid cap ~1.50 €

## Setup
Account: ads.google.com with talecraftmanagement@gmail.com (needs billing: card or invoice). Campaign type: Search. No Performance Max, no Display Network, no search partners.
Location: Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen (presence, not interest). Language: Finnish + English. Daily budget 2.50 €. Bidding: Maximise clicks with max CPC 1.50 €.
Conversion: Amelia booking-start event (already firing to the pixel) mirrored to Google via GTM, plus the lead form page. Landing: dicedungeon.fi/booking/ (consumer) and dicedungeon.fi/pricing/#team (company group).
