000b/000c Trailer (Tue 20 Oct, Facebook + TikTok):
Made a trailer for the Dice Dungeon. It's a private game room in Töölö, and the tablet by the table changes the lights and sound to fit whatever you're playing. If you want to try it, booking's at dicedungeon.fi

01 Scene shift (26 Oct):
This is my favourite part of the room. One tap on the tablet and the whole place goes from cosy tavern to dungeon, lights and sound together. Took a while to get right. Worth it.

02 Meet Tom (28 Oct):
Meet Tom, one of the Game Masters you can book with the room. He writes screenplays for a living and has been running games for over ten years: D&D, Call of Cthulhu, Star Wars, in Swedish, English or Finnish. Give him a backstory and he'll use it.

03 Pikkujoulut (30 Oct):
Planning pikkujoulut? We do a five-hour game night with a Game Master for groups of 8–10, in a private room with the lights and sound running the whole time. 600 € for the group. November Fridays are going quickly, so drop me a line at booking@talecraftgames.com
Pikkujoulut vielä suunnittelematta? Laita viestiä!

04 The table (2 Nov):
We had the table made for this room. 2.5 metres of velvet, LED lights along the edge, magnetic side tables and cup holders. Eight people fit comfortably, ten if you like each other.

05 Game shelf (4 Nov):
Not everyone wants to play D&D, and that's fine. There are 50+ board games on the shelf and a proper poker set, so most groups find something. Honestly, the hardest part is picking.

06 Subscription (6 Nov):
If you've got a campaign going, there's a subscription for that. One 6-hour session a month for 100 € instead of 200, and a missed month rolls over. Six months at a time, and there are only five spots.

07 Sound (9 Nov):
Sound on for this one. Rain and thunder, a crackling fire or just quiet music, whatever fits the moment. The GM runs it all from the tablet.

08 Meet Chris (11 Nov):
This is Chris, our other Game Master. Archaeologist turned social worker, running games for over ten years, and he builds his worlds out of old folklore. Fair warning, there will be puns. D&D, Mörk Borg, Monster of the Week, in English or Finnish.

09 Birthdays (13 Nov):
Birthday coming up? You can have the whole room for six hours. Bring your own games or use ours, put your own playlist on, and it's just your group. 200 €, so 20 € each if there are ten of you.

10 The door (16 Nov):
How it works: you ring the bell, we show you around and hand you the key. Then the room's yours for the night.

11 First-timers (18 Nov):
Never played D&D? Totally fine. The Game Master explains everything as you go and you get a ready-made character. A few hours in, you'll have strong opinions about your wizard.

12 December (20 Nov):
Heads up, December weekends are starting to fill. Free dates are in the calendar at dicedungeon.fi, and pikkujoulu groups can just email booking@talecraftgames.com
Joulukuun viikonloput alkavat täyttyä.
